Nilgiris Economic Dialogue

The Nilgiris Economic Dialogue by the Confederation of Indian Industry (CII) Tamil Nadu is an initiative that brings together leaders from diverse worlds — Industry, Government, Academia, Planning & Development Bodies — to engage on matters of importance for economic, social and equitable growth towards an optimistic future, through topics of relevance.

  • A 3-day initiative
  • Happening at the Queen of Hills, “Ooty”
  • Nestled in the world heritage natural ecosystem of the Western Ghats

Tracks

Four tracks, three days — each one a conversation about where India goes next.

Track 01

Data, Tech & Digital Economy

Strengthening Digital Frontiers

Exploring the interplay between technology, economics and society in the digital age — and how India can build the data, digital public infrastructure and skills to lead it.

Track 02

Geopolitics & Deglobalization

Trade Winds of Change: Strategies for an Evolving World

Navigating de-globalisation, shifting trade alliances and geopolitical realignments — and what they mean for India’s strategic partnerships and its place in the emerging world order.

Track 03

Resource Efficiency & Circularity

Closing the Loop: Strategies for Sustainable Growth

Join the discourse on resource efficiency and circularity, where we unravel the magic of turning waste into wealth and forging a path towards a regenerative and sustainable economy.

Track 04

Inclusive Growth

Growth for All: Strategies for Equitable Progress

Embark on a visionary journey towards inclusive growth, where diverse perspectives converge to forge pathways that uplift communities, foster prosperity and ensure no one is left behind.

Nilgiris Impact Challenge 2026

Hackathon

Young innovators, real district challenges, and a stage at NED 2026.

A district-governed innovation hackathon

Practical, scalable solutions for the Nilgiris

As part of NED 2026, CII in collaboration with the Nilgiris District Administration is organising the Nilgiris Impact Challenge 2026 — a district-governed, solution-oriented innovation hackathon led by the Nilgiris District Collectorate. The challenge engages young innovators in developing practical and scalable solutions to key developmental challenges of the Nilgiris district.

Institutions are invited to nominate five high-potential students (UG / PG / Doctoral) from diverse disciplines. Participants collaborate in Coimbatore during the first week of February 2026, and shortlisted teams present their solutions at NED 2026 in Ooty.

All participants receive certificates co-signed by the District Collector and CII, and all related expenses are borne by CII.
